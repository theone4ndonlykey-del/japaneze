DID YOU KNOW?

Fun facts about Japan

The Shinkansen high-speed train travels at speeds of up to 320 km/h and, on average, is delayed by less than one minute per year.

Mount Fuji (3,776 m) is the highest peak in Japan and has been regarded as a sacred mountain for hundreds of years.

Kyoto itself has around 1,600 Buddhist temples and 400 Shinto shrines.

There is no tipping culture when dining in Japan—good service is simply the standard.
